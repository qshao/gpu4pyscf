All XYZ files here are deterministic synthetic fixed test geometries in Angstrom, not experimentally optimized structures. They freeze workflow and numerical stress inputs; no experimental accuracy is asserted. Existing organic, water-cluster, and gly geometries retain their archive coordinates and are hashed at registry creation. Capacity water rungs are selected from actual CPU AO inventory before admission; they are not inferred from filenames. Periodic lattices are fixed synthetic benchmark cells.
